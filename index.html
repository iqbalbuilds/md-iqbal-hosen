About

I am a mechanical engineer who has spent most of my career where steel is made. For sixteen years I have run continuous casting machine operations in Bangladesh, leading shifts of more than 120 people and keeping output close to 400 tonnes a day. The work taught me that good decisions come from disciplined systems: clear maintenance protocols, honest data and people who know what to do when something goes wrong.

Alongside the plant, I studied electrical and electronic engineering (B.Sc., UITS Dhaka) and went deeper into how people decide. Today I write, teach and build digital workflows that bring the same engineering rigour to AI tools and behavioural science, mainly in Bengali.
